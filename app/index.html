核心关联方向稳定，大小取决于测量口径。The direction is stable; magnitude depends on measurement.

主模型使用有序 Logit，并控制性别、年龄、学历、收入、驾龄和驾驶频率。OR 大于 1 表示更高的 T/V 与更高购买溢价意愿的排序相关。The primary ordered-logit model controls for gender, age, education, income, driving experience, and driving frequency. An OR above 1 indicates an association with a higher willingness-to-pay category.

如何读这个结果How to read this result

统计模型负责回答研究问题，机器学习负责检查变量组合在未见折中的排序预测能力。两者结果方向一致，但机器学习没有替代有序 Logit 的解释角色。The statistical model answers the research question; machine learning checks ordinal prediction on held-out folds. The directions are compatible, but ML does not replace the interpretation of ordered logit.
