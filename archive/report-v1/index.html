基于 622 份问卷，分析消费者对智能驾驶安全性、功能价值的评价与支付溢价意愿的关系，并比较有序 Logit 和随机森林的预测表现。An analysis of 622 survey responses examining how ratings of driving safety and feature value relate to willingness to pay a premium. Ordered logit and random forest models are also compared using cross-validation.

方法与来源Methods and sources

01

变量定义Variable definitions

题项、组合变量、控制变量和缺失值处理规则见 src/data_schema.py 与 src/config.py。Item mappings, composites, controls and missing-value rules are defined in src/data_schema.py and src/config.py.

02

模型设定Model specification

有序 Logit 估计直接关联；间接路径分别采用 OLS。T、V 保持 1–5 分原尺度，控制变量按类别编码。Ordered logit estimates direct associations; OLS models are fitted separately for indirect paths. T and V retain their 1–5 scales, and controls are category-coded.

03

交叉验证Cross-validation

有序 Logit、随机森林和多数类基线使用相同的五折分层划分，随机种子为 42。Ordered logit, random forest and the majority baseline share the same five stratified folds, using seed 42.

04

预测设定Prediction settings

随机森林使用 200 棵树、最大深度 6；SHAP 解释扩展随机森林预测 Y≥4 的概率。Random forests use 200 trees with maximum depth 6. SHAP explains the extended forest’s predicted probability of Y≥4.

研究局限：问卷为横截面便利样本，Y 衡量支付溢价意愿，未记录实际购买行为。T、V 为题项组合指标，未经外部量表验证；V 与 Y 存在语义重叠。分析未预注册，题项组合曾在同一数据上探索，交叉验证未覆盖此前的选题过程。间接路径使用评分连续化的 OLS 近似，不建立因果关系或时间顺序。有序 Logit 的比例优势假设尚未专项检验。Limitations: this is a cross-sectional convenience sample. Y measures willingness to pay a premium, rather than actual purchases. T and V are item-mean measures without external scale validation, and V overlaps conceptually with Y. The analysis was not preregistered. Item combinations were explored on the same data; cross-validation does not cover that earlier selection process. Indirect paths use an OLS approximation with ratings treated as continuous and do not establish causality or temporal order. The proportional-odds assumption has not been tested separately.
